## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a sanctuary of dignity, reassurance, and intuitive warmth for elderly individuals and their care circles. Rejecting cold clinical telemetry, diagnostic intimidation, and frantic alerting patterns, the aesthetic combines timeless editorial warmth with high-comfort accessibility. 

The design narrative is anchored in **Sensory Warmth & Quiet Tactility**. Interfaces feel physical, inviting, and human—reminiscent of heavy archival stationery, comforting domestic environments, and sunlit morning spaces. Every touchpoint prioritizes immediate clarity, psychological calm, and natural sight-flow, eliminating cognitive fatigue for both aging adults and their adult children coordinating daily care.

### Core Principles
- **Compassionate Plainspokenness:** Information architecture is grounded in simple human realities. Technical, clinical, or algorithmic phrasing is strictly prohibited. Labels reflect lived routines: *Morning Routine*, *Doctor Visits*, *Prescriptions*, *Care Alerts*, and *Daily Medicine*.
- **Tactile Legibility:** Hierarchy is communicated through natural tone balance, deliberate weight, and high typographic scale ratios rather than aggressive visual decoration or visual clutter.
- **Atmospheric Restfulness:** The space breathes with intentional emptiness, generous padding, and gentle contrast boundaries, ensuring visual stillness across all operational modes.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from organic earth pigments, sunbaked clay, raw wheat, and warm unbleached parchment. In strict observance of system constraints, cool spectra (greens, blues, cyans, teals, and cold grays) are completely absent. All neutral and functional shades maintain a golden-umber undertone.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#8C4A2F` - Deep Terra-cotta):** Anchors primary calls-to-action, key focal points, active state highlights, and structural emphasis. Its darker shade (`#783820` - Roasted Umber) provides confident contrast on active tap states and high-priority indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C88132` - Honey Amber):** Delivers supportive warmth, indicating completed routines, soft notifications, schedule milestones, and uplifting reminders.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#783820` - Roasted Umber):** Reserved for grounding active states, tactile interactive outlines, and high-emphasis focal moments.
- **Canvas & Background Surfaces (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Alabaster / `#F5EFEB` - Soft Linen Ivory):** Foundational surface layers that bathe the interface in soft natural warmth, preventing screen glare and eye strain.
- **Surface Cards & Sheets (`#FFFFFF` to `#FCFAF6` - Warm Milk):** Discrete resting planes for distinct activities and care notices, framed with gentle warm stone perimeters (`#EADECE`).
- **Typographic Slate (`#241B18` - Warm Espresso Slate):** The singular high-contrast foundation for all headline and body text, providing deep contrast against warm ivory without the harshness of pitch black.
- **Secondary Muted Text (`#635752` - Earth Taupe):** Handles contextual subheadings, operational timings, and supporting metadata with clear legibility.
- **Status & Alerts:** Critical care reminders use an intense Terra-cotta Rust (`#A1351D`), while advisory notifications utilize warm Honey Amber (`#C88132`). No cool-spectrum colors are permitted for confirmations or operational status.

## Typography

Typography balances human literary elegance with senior-accessible legibility. The interplay pairs the graceful warmth of Playfair Display with the optical clarity and open apertures of Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Sizing and Spacing Rules
- **Base Senior Reading Scale:** Core reading copy starts at an accessible `18px` (`body-lg`) with an expansive `28px` line height. No interactive or essential instructional copy may drop below `16px`. Secondary caption roles are restricted to `14px` and must never convey primary health actions.
- **Letter Spacing:** Plus Jakarta Sans runs with generous positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.03em`) across body and label roles to maximize letter separation and minimize character collisions for users with declining visual acuity.
- **Headers:** Playfair Display introduces a comforting, human presence to section milestones, daily greetings, and key card headlines, infusing an editorial quality that removes the sterile feel of standard medical portals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an open, uncluttered fluid-responsive grid engineered around single-focus task architecture. Cluttered dashboards packed with competing modules are discarded in favor of clear sequential steps and spacious visual lanes.

### Responsive Behaviors & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (Up to 768px):** Single-column layout. Canvas margins set to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), gutter spacing at `1rem`. Core interactive blocks take 100% width with a minimum touch height of `56px` to ensure effortless targeting.
- **Tablet (769px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid structure. Outer margins increase to `2rem`. Content cards group logically into maximum 2-column paired arrangements (e.g., *Daily Medicine* alongside *Family Notes*).
- **Desktop (1025px+):** Max-width content boundary capped at `1200px` centered within the viewport to preserve reading comfort. 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Elements span wide tracks (6, 8, or 12 columns), maintaining open negative space across the periphery.

### Spacing Philosophy
Interfaces maintain consistent vertical rhythm using multiples of `0.25rem` (4px base factor), with standard component spacing resting on `space-md` (20px) and `space-lg` (32px). Sections never crowd one another; generous breathing space is essential to prevent cognitive overwhelm.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic floating drop-shadows and cold multi-tiered z-indexes. Visual layering is communicated via physical tonal stacking, warm ivory surfaces, and delicate stone borders.

### Surface Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Rendered in `#FAF7F2` (Warm Alabaster) or `#F5EFEB` (Soft Linen Ivory). This represents the deep physical counterpane.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Rendered in pure `#FFFFFF` or `#FCFAF6` (Warm Milk). These surfaces rise subtly above the canvas using a crisp, 1px structural perimeter in warm stone (`#EADECE` or `#E5DCD0`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Dialogs & Sheets):** Elevated containers retain the `#FCFAF6` surface tint paired with a soft, warm ambient feathering: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(36, 27, 24, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 27, 24, 0.03)`. The shadow is tinted with warm espresso rather than charcoal black, preserving the sunlit interior quality.

## Shapes

Every container, touch boundary, and interactive surface employs broad, organic radii to evoke safety, friendliness, and tactile comfort. There are no sharp corners or clinical edges.

### Radius Conventions
- **Standard UI Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** Utilize a generous `0.5rem` (8px) to `1rem` (16px) perimeter, creating friendly contact points for fingers and pointers.
- **Cards, Panels, and Content Blocks:** Employ `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for distinct card envelopes, creating a soft, welcoming feel.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets:** Feature large continuous corners at `2rem` (32px), creating calm, pillow-like overlays that sit gently atop the screen.

## Components

Components are optimized for immediate comprehension, forgiving target margins, and serene visual clarity.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Buttons:** Solid Deep Terra-cotta (`#8C4A2F`) background with Warm Milk (`#FFFFFF`) bold text (`label-lg`). Minimum height is `56px` with horizontal padding of `space-lg` (`2rem`). Corners use `rounded-lg` (`1rem`). On press, the background shifts smoothly to Roasted Umber (`#783820`).
- **Secondary Buttons:** Linen surface (`#F5EFEB`) bordered by Warm Stone (`#EADECE`), displaying Deep Warm Espresso text (`#241B18`). Provides an easy alternative action without competing for attention.

### Top Navigation
- Stripped of dense toolbars, micro-icons, and diagnostic badges. Features a clean logo mark in Deep Espresso, accompanied by plainspoken text links (*Today*, *Daily Medicine*, *Care Alerts*, *Family Notes*). Profile access is represented by a single warm portrait circle with a 2px stone border.

### Cards (Care & Routine Cards)
- Formed with a `#FCFAF6` surface, bounded by a 1.5px `#EADECE` stone border, with `rounded-xl` corners. 
- Internal padding is generous (`space-lg` / `2rem`). Headlines are rendered in Playfair Display (`headline-md`) with plain labels (*Morning Routine*, *Doctor Visits*). Status indicators inside cards use simple human tags with soft Honey Amber accents (`#C88132`).

### Chips & Action Pill Filters
- Height of `44px` for easy tap navigation. Resting state uses a Soft Linen Ivory background (`#F5EFEB`) with Earth Taupe typography (`#635752`). 
- Active state transitions to Deep Terra-cotta (`#8C4A2F`) fill with warm ivory text. No micro icons or complex drop-downs.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Large target fields with a minimum height of `58px`, rendered with a `#FFFFFF` fill and a 1.5px `#E5DCD0` perimeter. 
- Text is sized at `body-lg` (`18px`) for effortless input review. Focus states transition the border smoothly to Deep Terra-cotta (`#8C4A2F`) with a 2px stroke, accompanied by a soft, warm 3px halo (`rgba(140, 74, 47, 0.12)`).

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Sized comfortably at `28px x 28px` with `0.375rem` rounded corners to ensure easy physical targeting.
- Unchecked state features an unadorned `#FFFFFF` surface with an `#EADECE` border. Checked state transitions to a solid Deep Terra-cotta (`#8C4A2F`) fill displaying a clean, warm white checkmark icon. Paired labels use `body-lg` text.

### Family Voice & Care Alert Banners
- Non-punitive, warm visual framing for urgent messages. Built using a light Honey Amber base (`#FDF6ED`) edged with a 1.5px amber border (`#D4904A`). Accompanied by a bold Terra-cotta indicator dot and plain everyday language: *"Mom has not confirmed her morning medicine yet."*
